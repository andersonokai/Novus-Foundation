About Us

Novus Foundation

Novus Innovation and Empowerment Foundation (operating publicly as Novus Foundation) is an impact-driven, non-profit innovation hub based in Ghana, dedicated to fostering sustainable social venture ecosystems and driving grassroots progress.

We believe that technology is the greatest equalizer of our time. Our organization is committed to bridging the gap between potential and opportunity through youth digital literacy, tech entrepreneurship, and community empowerment.

Operating primarily through the Novus Skills Hub, we design and deploy practical, localized learning programs tailored to rural and peri-urban communities. We equip young people and women in underserved regions with essential digital literacy and mentorship needed to create sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission & Vision

What drives us

Mission

To bridge the digital divide by equipping youth, women, and grassroots organizations in underserved communities with essential digital skills, entrepreneurial mentorship, and practical technology needed to build sustainable livelihoods and drive systemic social change.

Where we are going

Vision

To become Africa’s leading catalyst for tech-driven social innovation, creating a future where every community has the digital tools, open ecosystems, and opportunities necessary to thrive in a rapidly evolving global economy.

Our Core Values

Innovation

Championing forward-thinking, adaptable technology to solve local challenges.

Collaboration

Believing that true systemic change is driven by open exchange and strategic partnerships.

Social Responsibility

Maintaining a deep commitment to uplifting underserved populations and technology for the public good.

Sustainability

Building capacity and support systems designed for long-term economic growth and resilience.

Diversity & Inclusion

Ensuring equitable access to technology and opportunity for women and marginalized communities.

Excellence

Holding our operations, training, and technical work to the highest standards.
